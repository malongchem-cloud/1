FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Practical answers about searching, ordering and working with our company.

How do I search for a product?

Enter the exact CAS number in the search bar. The site normalizes formatting and opens the matching product page.

Can you supply custom quantities or grades?

Please send an inquiry with product, CAS number, quantity and target specification. Our team will confirm availability and packaging.

What industries use your intermediates?

Our products are used in pharmaceuticals, agrochemicals, liquid crystal materials, fragrances and fine chemical synthesis.

How should I request a quote?

Use the inquiry form on any product page, email info@malongchem.com, or send a WhatsApp message to +86 135 8453 5752.

Do you support custom synthesis?

Yes. We support new product development, process optimization, pilot trials and scale-up production for selected intermediates.
