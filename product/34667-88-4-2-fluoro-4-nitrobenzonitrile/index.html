Applications & downstream uses

2-fluoro-4-nitrobenzonitrile is a fluoroaryl nitrile building block for fluoroaryl nitrile intermediates for agrochemical fungicides (Bixafen, Boscalid) and fluoroaniline APIs (Dasatinib class).

Fluoroaryl nitriles are practical building blocks for agrochemical fungicides and fluoroaniline pharmaceutical routes, including kinase inhibitor research.
