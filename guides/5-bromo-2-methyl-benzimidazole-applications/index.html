5-Bromo-2-methyl-1H-benzimidazole (CAS 1964-77-8): Applications and Supplier Guide

CAS 1964-77-8 is the registry number for 5-bromo-2-methyl-1H-benzimidazole, a brominated methylbenzimidazole intermediate for pharmaceutical building blocks.

Product overview

We supply 5-bromo-2-methyl-1H-benzimidazole (CAS 1964-77-8) as a high-purity benzimidazole building block. The catalogue specification is 98% min purity; formula and molecular weight are C8H7BrN2 and 211.06.

Applications & downstream uses

5-bromo-2-methyl-1H-benzimidazole is a benzimidazole building block for benzimidazole APIs, including proton pump inhibitors (Omeprazole, Esomeprazole), Dabigatran etexilate and anthelmintics (Albendazole, Mebendazole).

Sourcing considerations

  • Verify the CAS number against the requested structure
  • Confirm purity, impurity profile and analytical method
  • Check packaging and minimum order quantity
  • Request COA and MSDS before shipment
