Chemical Intermediate Quality, Packaging and MOQ Guide

Quality confirmation starts before the order. This guide lists the documents and product details buyers should confirm with a chemical intermediate supplier.

Core documents to check

  • Certificate of Analysis (COA) with CAS number, assay and relevant impurities
  • Formula, molecular weight and physical form
  • Purity or assay method used by the supplier
  • Batch number and packaging information

Packaging and MOQ

MOQ depends on the product and packaging format. Confirm the available package sizes, container type and required documentation before placing an order.

Shipping and storage

Ask about shelf life, storage conditions and transport restrictions. These details are especially important for international orders.

Frequently asked questions

What is a typical MOQ?

MOQ varies by product and packaging. Contact us with the CAS number and target quantity for the current minimum.

Can I request a sample before a bulk order?

Share the product and quantity with our sales team to confirm sample availability and any applicable terms.

What documents are usually available?

COA, batch information and packaging details are the most common documents. Specific regulatory documents should be discussed with the supplier.
