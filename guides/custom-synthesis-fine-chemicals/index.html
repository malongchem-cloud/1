Custom Synthesis and Fine Chemical Supply from Changzhou Malong Chemicals

Not every target intermediate is available in a standard catalog. Custom synthesis and fine chemical supply help research and production teams obtain the exact building block they need.

When custom synthesis makes sense

Custom synthesis is useful when a target intermediate is not in the catalog, when a different substitution pattern is required, or when the project needs scale-up from research to commercial quantities.

Information to share

  • Target CAS number or structure
  • Quantity range and purity target
  • Reference to the downstream API or application if available
  • Timeline, packaging and destination requirements

How the process starts

Share the specification by email or WhatsApp. The team will confirm feasibility, estimated lead time and required documentation before quoting.

Frequently asked questions

Do you take on custom synthesis for research quantities?

Share the target structure, quantity and purity; feasibility and lead time can be confirmed by the team.

What information do you need for a custom inquiry?

A CAS number or structure, target quantity, purity and timeline are the most important details.

Can custom synthesis be scaled up?

Discuss the target quantity and timeline with our team to confirm the scale-up route and available capacity.
