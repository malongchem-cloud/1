Agrochemical Intermediates: Product Families and Procurement Guide

Agrochemical synthesis uses a broad range of aromatic and heterocyclic intermediates. Understanding which product families map to fungicide, insecticide and herbicide chemistry helps buyers send more accurate inquiries.

Product families

Pyridine, pyrimidine, aniline and nitrile intermediates are common in agrochemical synthesis. The right building block depends on the target molecule and the functional groups required.

Representative applications

  • Pyridine intermediates are used in neonicotinoid insecticide chemistry
  • Halogenated anilines support fungicide families such as Bixafen and Boscalid
  • Pyrimidine intermediates are used in sulfonylurea herbicide and antimicrobial chemistry

Frequently asked questions

What intermediates are most common in agrochemical synthesis?

Halogenated anilines, pyridines, pyrimidines and nitriles are all common, depending on the target class.
