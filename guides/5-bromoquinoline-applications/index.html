5-Bromoquinoline (CAS 4964-71-0): Applications and Supplier Guide

5-Bromoquinoline is one of our promoted quinoline building blocks. This guide summarizes the product specification, typical application areas and the information to confirm before ordering.

What is 5-bromoquinoline?

5-Bromoquinoline (CAS 4964-71-0) is a brominated quinoline building block. Its molecular formula is C9H6BrN and its molecular weight is 208.05.

Typical application areas

The product is used as a halogenated quinoline intermediate for antimalarial and antimicrobial quinoline API families, including Primaquine and Chloroquine chemistry, and for further functionalization in medicinal chemistry research.

Specification checklist

  • CAS 4964-71-0
  • Formula C9H6BrN
  • Molecular weight 208.05
  • Purity 98% min, with current batch COA to be confirmed by the supplier

What is the difference between 5-bromoquinoline and 8-bromoquinoline?

The bromine is attached at a different position of the quinoline ring, which changes the substitution pattern and downstream chemistry. The CAS number must be checked to avoid confusion.
