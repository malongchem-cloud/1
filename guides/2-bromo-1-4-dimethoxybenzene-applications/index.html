2-Bromo-1,4-dimethoxybenzene (CAS 25245-34-5): Applications and Supplier Guide

CAS 25245-34-5 is the registry number for 2-bromo-1,4-dimethoxybenzene, a halogenated dimethoxybenzene intermediate for cross-coupling and materials chemistry.

Product overview

We supply 2-bromo-1,4-dimethoxybenzene (CAS 25245-34-5) as a high-purity halogenated aromatic building block. The catalogue specification is 98% min purity; formula and molecular weight are C8H9BrO2 and 217.06.

Applications & downstream uses

2-bromo-1,4-dimethoxybenzene is a halogenated aromatic building block for polymethoxyaryl building blocks for pharmaceutical APIs and OLED/OPV monomers (MEH-PPV derivatives).
