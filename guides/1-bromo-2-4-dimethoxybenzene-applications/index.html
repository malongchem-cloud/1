1-Bromo-2,4-dimethoxybenzene (CAS 17715-69-4): Applications and Supplier Guide

CAS 17715-69-4 is the registry number for 1-bromo-2,4-dimethoxybenzene, a halogenated aromatic building block with methoxy-substituted aryl chemistry.

Product overview

We supply 1-bromo-2,4-dimethoxybenzene (CAS 17715-69-4) as a high-purity halogenated aromatic building block. The catalogue specification is 98% min purity; formula and molecular weight are C8H9BrO2 and 217.06.

Applications & downstream uses

1-Bromo-2,4-dimethoxybenzene is a halogenated aromatic building block for polymethoxyaryl building blocks for pharmaceutical APIs and OLED/OPV monomers (MEH-PPV derivatives).

Quality and sourcing notes

  • Check purity, appearance and residual solvent specification
  • Confirm packaging, MOQ and shipping terms
  • Request COA and MSDS with the quotation
