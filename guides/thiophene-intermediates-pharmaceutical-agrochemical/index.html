Thiophene Intermediates for Pharmaceuticals and Agrochemicals

Thiophene and halothiophene intermediates are useful heterocyclic building blocks for pharmaceutical and agrochemical synthesis, especially where sulfur-containing heterocycles are required.

Why the product family matters

Thiophene derivatives support thieno[3,2-c]pyridine drug chemistry such as Clopidogrel and Ticlopidine and thiophene-based agrochemical intermediates.

Representative applications

Halogenated thiophenes such as 3-bromo-2-chlorothiophene provide reactive handles for further substitution, cross-coupling and heterocycle functionalization.

Procurement checklist

  • Confirm the exact halogenation and substitution pattern
  • Check purity and isomer distribution
  • Confirm packaging, scale and lead time
  • Request COA, MSDS and analytical documentation

Frequently asked questions

What are thiophene intermediates used for?

They are used as heterocyclic building blocks in pharmaceutical chemistry, including thieno[3,2-c]pyridine routes, and in agrochemical synthesis.

Which APIs are associated with this chemistry?

The catalogue references Clopidogrel and Ticlopidine chemistry; suitability for a specific route must be confirmed by the buyer.

How should I specify a thiophene intermediate?

Provide the CAS number, halogenation pattern, purity, quantity, packaging and destination.
