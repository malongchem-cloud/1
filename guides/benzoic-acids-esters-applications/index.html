Benzoic Acids and Esters for Pharmaceutical and Agrochemical Synthesis

Benzoic acids and esters are versatile aromatic building blocks used across pharmaceutical, agrochemical and specialty chemical projects. The substitution pattern determines how the intermediate is used in the next synthetic step.

Why the product family matters

Benzoic acids and esters support SGLT2 inhibitor chemistry, iodinated pharmaceutical and contrast intermediates, aminobenzoate drugs and chlorobenzoic acid herbicide research.

Representative applications

The catalogue includes iodinated benzoate building blocks used in SGLT2 inhibitor routes such as Dapagliflozin and Empagliflozin, as well as aminobenzoate chemistry associated with Benzocaine and Procaine-class APIs and agrochemical amide synthesis.

Specifications to confirm

  • Exact CAS number and substitution pattern
  • Acid or ester form required for the route
  • Purity, residual solvent and analytical method
  • Packaging, MOQ and delivery terms

Frequently asked questions

What products are included in this family?

The family includes benzoic acids, benzoate esters and halogenated aromatic acid or ester building blocks used in pharmaceutical and agrochemical synthesis.

Which applications use these intermediates?

Typical examples include SGLT2 inhibitor routes, iodinated contrast or pharmaceutical intermediates, aminobenzoate APIs and agrochemical amide chemistry.

How should I specify a benzoic acid or ester?

Provide the CAS number, substitution pattern, acid or ester form, target purity, quantity and destination.

Can you provide COA and MSDS?

Yes, certificates of analysis and safety documentation can be provided with the quotation on request.
