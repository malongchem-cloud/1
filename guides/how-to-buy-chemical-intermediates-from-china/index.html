How to Buy Chemical Intermediates from China: A Practical Guide

Buying chemical intermediates from China becomes much smoother when the specification is clear and the CAS number is verified. This guide covers the information buyers should prepare before contacting a supplier.

Define your specification first

Before contacting suppliers, write down the CAS number, target purity, quantity, packaging preference and destination country. A precise specification helps avoid quotation drift and shortens the confirmation cycle.

What to include in an inquiry

  • CAS number and product name
  • Quantity in grams or kilograms
  • Target purity and required documents such as COA or HPLC data
  • Packaging type and estimated shipping terms
  • Timeline and destination country

From quotation to order

After receiving the quotation, confirm sample policy, MOQ, payment terms, packaging and delivery lead time. Keep the CAS number and specification in the order confirmation so both sides are aligned.
